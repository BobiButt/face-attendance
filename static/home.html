✨ What This Project Does

👤 Face Registration

Register a person by capturing their face once. Stores a 512-d embedding — no raw images saved.

🎥 Live Recognition

Point your webcam and it recognizes you automatically every 1.5 seconds — no button press needed.

📋 Auto Attendance Log

Every successful recognition logs the name + timestamp. Smart 30-second deduplication.

🔒 Privacy-First

Face data is stored as a numerical vector, not a photo. Cannot be reversed back to a face.

🚀 How To Use

  1. Go to Register, allow camera, type name, capture, click Register.
  2. Open Recognize, click Start Recognition, show your face.
  3. The overlay turns green with your name when you're recognized.
  4. Open Attendance to see the full history (auto-refreshes every 5s).
